  • 🃏
    Each player gets 10 cards. You see only your own card each round.
  • 🎯
    All 13 stats are available every round — batting and bowling. Pick your strongest stat!
  • 👆
    The challenger picks a stat. All others tap the blinking stat to confirm.

    1v1: Win a round = +1 win. Most wins after 10 rounds wins the match! In 3-4 player mode: 1st=3pts · 2nd=2pts · 3rd=1pt · 4th=0pts.
