📋 HOW TO PLAY
  • 🏏
    Create a room or join with a code, or use Quick Match to find a random opponent.
  • 🃏
    Both players receive 10 cards. You see your cards; your opponent's are hidden.
  • 👆
    Player 1 picks a stat each round. Player 2 waits to see which stat was chosen.
  • ⬆️
    Higher is better for Runs, Wickets, 6s, 100s, Strike Rate, Catches etc.
  • ⬇️
    Lower is better for Economy Rate.
  • 🎳
    Best Bowling: More wickets wins. Equal wickets → fewer runs wins.
  • 🏆
    Most points after 10 rounds wins the match!
